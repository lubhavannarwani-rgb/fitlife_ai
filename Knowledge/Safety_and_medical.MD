# Safety and Medical Guidance

## When to see a doctor
Consult a doctor or registered dietitian before changing diet or exercise if you have diabetes, heart or kidney disease, high blood pressure, are pregnant, have an eating disorder history, or take regular medication.

## Allergies
Food allergy filtering in an app cannot guarantee safety. Always read ingredient labels and watch for cross-contamination, especially with nuts, milk, eggs, soy, gluten, fish and shellfish.

## Limits of this app
This app gives general fitness and nutrition guidance. It is not medical advice and does not replace a qualified professional.
