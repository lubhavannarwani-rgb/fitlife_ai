# Fibre and Digestion

## Daily fibre intake
Adults are commonly advised to eat roughly 25 to 38 grams of fibre per day. Very high fibre intakes, such as well over 50 grams, can cause bloating, gas and discomfort, especially if the increase is sudden.

## Increasing fibre safely
Increase fibre gradually over a few weeks and drink enough water. Spread fibre-rich foods like dals, oats, vegetables, fruit and seeds across meals instead of eating them all at once.
