# Training Principles

## Progressive overload
Muscles adapt when the workload slowly increases. Add reps, sets, difficulty or resistance gradually over weeks. Track workouts so you can see progress.

## Rest between sets and sessions
For strength and muscle gain, resting about 60 to 120 seconds between sets is common. Allow 48 hours before training the same muscle group hard again. Take at least one or two rest days per week.

## Warm-up and cooldown
A 5 minute warm-up with light movement and joint circles prepares the body and lowers injury risk. A short cooldown with stretching and slow breathing helps the body return to rest.

## Training without equipment
Bodyweight training can build strength. Make exercises harder using slower tempo, pauses, single-leg variations, elevated feet, or more reps. Examples include push-up variations, split squats, and glute bridges.

## Exercise form and pain
Use controlled form. Stop an exercise if you feel sharp pain. People with injuries or medical conditions should consult a doctor or physiotherapist before training.
