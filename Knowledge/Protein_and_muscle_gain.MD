# Protein and Muscle Gain

## How much protein for muscle gain
Sports nutrition guidance commonly suggests about 1.4 to 2.0 grams of protein per kilogram of body weight per day for people who train with resistance exercise. Going far above this range usually gives no extra muscle benefit. Spreading protein over 3 to 5 meals is a practical approach.

## Calorie surplus for lean gain
To gain muscle with limited fat gain, a small surplus of roughly 200 to 300 kcal above maintenance is a common starting point. If body weight does not rise by about 0.25 to 0.5 kg per month, increase calories slightly. If weight rises faster, reduce the surplus.

## Meal timing
Total daily protein and calories matter more than exact timing. Having a protein-containing meal within a few hours before or after training is a simple habit that works for most people.
