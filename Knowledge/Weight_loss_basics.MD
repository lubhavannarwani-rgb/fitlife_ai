# Weight Loss Basics

## Rate of weight loss
A gradual loss of about 0.25 to 0.75 kg per week is usually considered sustainable. Very low calorie diets can cause muscle loss, tiredness and nutrient gaps.

## Protect muscle while losing weight
Keep protein high, around 1.6 grams per kilogram of body weight, continue strength training, and avoid large calorie cuts. A moderate deficit of about 300 to 500 kcal below maintenance is common.

## Activity and steps
Daily walking adds to calorie use without heavy fatigue. Combine it with strength training and a consistent eating pattern.
