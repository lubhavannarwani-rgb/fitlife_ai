# Hydration and Sleep

## Hydration
Water needs vary with body size, climate and activity. A common practical guide is to drink regularly through the day and aim for pale yellow urine. Many active adults drink about 2 to 3 litres of fluids a day, and more in hot weather or long sessions.

## Sleep and recovery
Most adults do best with about 7 to 9 hours of sleep. Poor sleep can reduce training quality, recovery and appetite control. A regular sleep schedule helps muscle recovery and results.
